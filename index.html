The project

Two cities. One quieter address.

Happy Homes sits on the spine that connects Cuttack and Bhubaneswar, in Trisulia. Close enough to both that your commute is a question of minutes. Far enough from either that the evening actually feels like the evening.

We took 2.80 acres of it and laid out 361 homes across four blocks. Basement, stilt and five floors above. Cars stay tucked into the stilt. Balconies open to north light. Configurations are 2 and 3 BHK, planned for the kind of family that wants room to breathe without paying for square feet it will never use.

More than 60 percent of the ground is kept open. A jogging track, a kids’ play area, a swimming pool, a badminton court, sit-outs under the trees. The community is built to walk through, not just live in.

FAQ

What configurations are available?
Four north facing configurations. Two variants of 2 BHK (Super built-up 1025 and 1040 sqft) and two variants of 3 BHK (Super built-up 1190 and 1270 sqft). Carpet, balcony and built-up sizes are listed in the Plans section.
What is the starting price?
2 BHK starts at ₹ 57 Lakhs. 3 BHK starts at ₹ 65 Lakhs. Final pricing varies by floor, block and unit. Submit the form for current pricing and active offers.
Where is the project located?
Trisulia, Cuttack, Odisha 754005. On the corridor between Cuttack and Bhubaneswar, 25 minutes from Biju Pattnaik International Airport, 3 minutes from Netaji SCB Bridge.
What amenities are included?
Over 50 amenities across Clubhouse and Outdoor categories. Swimming pool, kids’ play area, jogging track, badminton court, gym, multipurpose hall, co-working lounge, yoga space, and more. The Amenities section above has the complete list.
Is home loan assistance available?
Yes. we have tie-ups with major nationalised and private banks. Our sales team will share approved loan options and rates when you enquire.
When is possession?
Possession timelines are confirmed at the time of booking based on the block and floor you choose. Construction is underway across the site. Our sales team will share the precise quarterly plan when you enquire.
